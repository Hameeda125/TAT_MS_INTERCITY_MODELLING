METHODOLOGICAL ACCOUNTING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The six predictive phases are:
STOP, IDLE, INCHING, ACC, DEC, CRUISE

Charging and refuelling are handled separately and are not predictive targets in the six-phase TS branch.

Therefore:

ICE TAT = six predictive phase time + excluded operational time

EV TAT = six predictive phase time + excluded operational time

Six-phase time is therefore not required to equal total TAT.

ROUTE-PROGRESS REPRESENTATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Telemetry intervals are allocated to route bins using interval-overlap allocation.

A telemetry interval may therefore contribute to more than one route bin.

This prevents sparse telemetry intervals from creating artificial empty route bins.

ACCELERATION VALIDATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Negative acceleration is physically valid and represents deceleration.
Therefore negative acceleration values are not treated as dataset errors.

EMPTY PREDICTIVE PHASE BINS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Empty predictive-phase bins are informational only and are not automatically treated as missing telemetry.
