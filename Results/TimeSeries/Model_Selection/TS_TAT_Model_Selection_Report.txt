INTERPRETATION NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAE, RMSE and R2 are reported phase-by-phase.
MAPE is not used as a primary metric because phase-time targets contain zero / near-zero values.
The six-phase aggregate represents driving-phase TAT only; EV charging time is excluded.
EV charging time remains a separate calculation in the final EV TAT reconstruction workflow.
Only six independent paired trips are available, so model differences remain preliminary.
This stage performs model comparison only; no hyperparameter tuning is performed.
